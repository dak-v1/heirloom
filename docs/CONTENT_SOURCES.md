# Content and source register

Every fact, photograph, translation and label in Heirloom, where it came from, and what still needs checking. Sources were consulted on **10 October 2026**.

## 1. Curation rules

- Stories and hotspots state only what the cited sources say. Where official sources disagree on a detail (an exact day, a rebuilding year), the app uses the agreed part (usually the year) or the National Heritage Board's figure, and the disagreement is listed in §5.
- Hotspots are tagged **Sourced fact** (with a link) or **What you can see** (an observation about the picture, not a historical claim).
- Photographs are shown with the date exactly as the archive gives it. The era timeline only offers eras for which a real photograph exists. Heirloom contains no AI-generated "historical" images.
- Narration is synthetic (device text-to-speech, or pre-generated ElevenLabs audio when configured). Soundscapes are synthesised in the browser and labelled illustrative.
- English versions are the curated reference. 中文 and Bahasa Melayu versions are AI-assisted **draft translations**, labelled in the app, awaiting review by fluent community reviewers. Tamil is not available yet and is shown as such.

**Licence obligations.** CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-SA images require attribution (title, creator, source, licence link) and an indication of changes. Changes made when displaying them:
- archival crops listed in the table above (to trim an album page or hide a handwritten date in the game);
- present-day photos are cropped to fit the site banner and trail cards and given a light sepia/desaturation tint, and the Today photo is shown blurred and darkened as the Past View background.

Those on-screen adaptations of CC BY-SA photos remain under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or the original version's licence). Archival images are displayed unaltered apart from the listed crops.

**Wording.** Some archive titles are colonial-era terms ("Klingalese temple", "Hindoo Temple", "Moslem Mosque"). They are shown as archive titles only, and the Kleingrothe image carries a note that the term is outdated.

## 4. Original artwork

Stamps, badges, postmark, orchid, shophouse divider, rubber-stamp illustration, passport cover/endpaper/identity page and both 3D models are original work generated by `scripts/assets/*` for this project. The logo mark (and the app icons and favicon made from it) is illustrated artwork supplied by the team. Typefaces converted to outlines in the artwork: Fraunces, Noto Serif SC, Noto Serif Tamil, Noto Naskh Arabic and Caveat (all SIL Open Font License, via Fontsource). The emblem shows simplified silhouettes of the three sites and is **not** based on any government crest. The passport states it is not an official travel document.
